### 1. SuperSplat WebGPU + LOD Upgrade (PlayCanvas)

**Source:** X.com timeline | **Author:** @PlayCanvas | **Time:** 2026-06-03 13:31 UTC

**Original text:**

> Major upgrades just landed in SuperSplat, the free and open source platform for 3D Gaussian splatting.
>
> Here is a 24-MILLION-Gaussian scan streaming live to a web browser. Near instant load time. Solid 60 fps.
>
> How? A new compute-based WebGPU renderer + automatic LOD

**Analysis:** SuperSplat (playcanvas/supersplat) is the core open-source tool for inspecting, editing, and publishing 3DGS scenes in the browser. This upgrade adds a compute-shader-based WebGPU renderer and automatic level-of-detail (LOD) system — enabling real-time visualization of scenes with 24M+ Gaussians, an order-of-magnitude improvement over prior browser renderers. This positions SuperSplat as a production-grade tool for 3DGS workflows, complementing the simpler [[splat-webgl]] viewer and the reference [[gaussian-impl]].

**GitNexus triage:** 4,720 nodes, 9,520 edges, 141 clusters, 300 flows — a mature, well-structured codebase.
