### 3. Agent-Reach — @self.dll

[[Agent-Reach]] (Panniantong/Agent-Reach) is an open-source Python toolkit that equips AI agents with zero-API-fee access to social and content platforms (Twitter/X, Reddit, YouTube, GitHub, Bilibili, Xiaohongshu, LinkedIn, and more).
